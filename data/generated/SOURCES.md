# Everyday vocabulary sources

The Everyday collection combines hand-curated entries with a larger imported set. Curated entries use newly written English definitions, Vietnamese meanings, and examples. Imported entries retain the source English definition and Vietnamese translation.

## Full personal-study lists

The following source files are included locally as candidate pools. Phrasal verbs are included only when the app's existing Zipf frequency data classifies them as “Phổ biến” or “Rất phổ biến” (Zipf 4.3+); idioms use a manually selected shortlist because most full idiom phrases have no exact Zipf entry. The same frequency labels and badge styling as the book's vocabulary pages are used. Duplicate headwords already present in the curated list are skipped. The source lists do not provide example sentences, so imported entries show the English definition and Vietnamese translation without an example.

- Project: [GitHub Vocabulary](https://github.com/tema4p/english-vocabulary)
- Files: [1,845 idioms](https://github.com/tema4p/english-vocabulary/blob/main/content/idioms/English_Idioms_in_Action_EN.txt) with [Vietnamese translations](https://github.com/tema4p/english-vocabulary/blob/main/content/idioms/English_Idioms_in_Action_VI.txt); [1,511 phrasal verbs](https://github.com/tema4p/english-vocabulary/blob/main/content/phrasal-verbs/English_Phrasal_Verbs_in_Action_EN.txt) with [Vietnamese translations](https://github.com/tema4p/english-vocabulary/blob/main/content/phrasal-verbs/English_Phrasal_Verbs_in_Action_VI.txt).

## Candidate discovery

- [MAGPIE corpus on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gsarti/magpie): used as a source of candidate idiomatic expressions and contextual usage patterns. The dataset card identifies the corpus as English, sense-annotated data based on the British National Corpus, and licenses it under CC BY 4.0. We do not copy its corpus sentences into the app. The expressions `across the board` and `above board` were visible in the corpus preview and were added as vocabulary candidates.
- MAGPIE citation: Haagsma, Hessel, Johan Bos, and Malvina Nissim (2020). “MAGPIE: A Large Corpus of Potentially Idiomatic Expressions.” LREC 2020, pp. 279–287. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.35).
